About the team

USRT is the premier student-led engineering and research club at Fresno State, dedicated to designing, building, and testing autonomous systems and robotics - from a self-driving golf cart to drones and UAVs.

We welcome all Fresno State students curious about autonomous systems, regardless of major or experience level. Members machine rover and/or drone chassis, write navigation code, and take builds out to solve real-world problems.
